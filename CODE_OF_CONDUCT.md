# atio Community Code of Conduct

Welcome to the atio community! We’re thrilled to have you here. To keep this space friendly, safe, and inclusive for everyone, we follow this Code of Conduct.

---

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in the atio community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, or experience level. We aim to treat each other with respect and kindness.

---

## Our Standards
When participating in atio, please:
- Be **respectful** and **considerate** to others.
- Avoid **harassment**, offensive language, or personal attacks.
- Use **inclusive language** and welcome diverse perspectives.
- Give **constructive feedback**, not discouraging criticism.
- Foster a **positive and supportive community**.

---

## Reporting Issues
If you experience or witness any behavior that violates this Code of Conduct:
1. Contact the maintainers of this repository.
2. All reports will be reviewed **confidentially**.
3. Possible actions include warnings, removal from discussions, or blocking if necessary.

---

## Enforcement
Community members are expected to follow this Code of Conduct at all times. Maintainers reserve the right to take action to ensure the community remains safe and welcoming.
